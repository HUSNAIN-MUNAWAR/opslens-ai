# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want OpsLens AI to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming project for contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give technical feedback with clarity and empathy.
- Focus discussion on the work, not the person.
- Share context, tradeoffs, and evidence when disagreeing.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Disruptive or bad-faith conduct in issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Reporting

Please report conduct concerns to `husnainbinmunawar@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed privately and handled in good faith.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code of conduct.
